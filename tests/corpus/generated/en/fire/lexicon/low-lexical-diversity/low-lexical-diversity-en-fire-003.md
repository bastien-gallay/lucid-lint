The team has a meeting every day, and every meeting starts with the agenda, but the agenda for each meeting is mostly a discussion of the last meeting. We discuss the same items, we take the same notes, and we decide to discuss the items again in the next meeting. The meeting is scheduled for one hour, the meeting always runs past the hour, and the calendar already shows another meeting right after this meeting. The team talks about action items, but the action items from this meeting look exactly like the action items from the last meeting, because nobody has time between meetings to do the items we discuss. Someone suggests we schedule a shorter meeting to decide how to shorten the meetings, and the team adds that meeting to the calendar. My notes from Monday's meeting match my notes from Tuesday's meeting; the same team, the same agenda, the same hour of talking. We meet to discuss, we discuss to decide, we decide to meet again, and the calendar fills with meetings about meetings while the actual items wait for a meeting that never comes.
