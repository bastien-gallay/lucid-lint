My commute starts at the station, and the station is crowded every morning, because every commuter takes the same train at the same time. I wait on the platform, the train arrives late, the doors open, and the crowded carriage swallows more people than it should. Some mornings the train is only five minutes late, and some mornings the train is twenty minutes late, but the train is never on time. Finding a seat is rare; most mornings I stand between other standing people, waiting for my station. The platform announcements promise the next train in three minutes, then six minutes, then the announcements stop promising anything. People push through the doors, people wait by the doors, people miss the doors entirely and wait on the platform for the next crowded train. My whole commute is waiting: waiting at the station, waiting on the platform, waiting for the doors, waiting for a seat that never comes. Every evening the same train carries the same people back, the carriage just as crowded, the doors just as slow, and every morning the commute begins again at the same crowded station.
