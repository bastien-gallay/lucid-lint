Every month I open the budget spreadsheet and the budget tells the same story: we spend more money than we planned to spend. The plan says save, the numbers say spend, and the bills keep the numbers honest. I cut costs where I can cut them, but every cost I cut, a new cost appears, and the new cost is always bigger than the cost I cut. The spreadsheet tracks the money in and the money out, and every month the money out beats the money in. We plan to save at the start of the month, we spend through the middle of the month, and we count the bills at the end of the month. Cutting the small costs saves small money, and the big bills never shrink, so the budget never balances. Next month I will make a new plan, a stricter budget, a leaner spreadsheet, and next month the bills will arrive anyway, the costs will grow anyway, and the money will go where the money always goes. The budget is not really a plan for saving money; the budget is a monthly record of spending it.
