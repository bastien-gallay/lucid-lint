# Getting Started with Sourdough

Baking bread at home is easier than it looks. You need flour, water, salt, and patience.

## Feeding Your Starter

A starter is a living culture. Feed it once a day with equal parts flour and water.

### Signs of a Healthy Starter

It should double in size within six hours and smell pleasantly sour, like yogurt.

## Mixing the Dough

Combine the starter with flour and water, then rest the mixture for thirty minutes before adding salt.
