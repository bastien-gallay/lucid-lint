# Caring for a Rescue Cat

A rescue cat needs time to feel safe. Give it a quiet room for the first few days.

## Food and Water

Offer small meals twice a day. Keep the water bowl away from the food bowl.

## Health Checks

Book a vet visit within the first week.

### Vaccinations

Ask the shelter which shots the cat has already received.

### Parasite Treatment

Most shelters treat for fleas, but confirm before you skip it.

## Settling In

After two weeks, most cats begin to explore the whole home on their own.
