Setting up your new printer takes about ten minutes.

First, unpack everything.

- Remove the blue tape from the paper tray.
- Plug in the power cable.

Then install the ink.

- Open the front cover.
  - The black cartridge goes on the left.
  - The color cartridge goes on the right.
- Close the cover and wait for the light to stop blinking.

Finally, print a test page to check that everything works.
